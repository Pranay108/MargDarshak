GFR 2017 RULE 144(i) STATUTORY TENDER CLAUSE
The supplied 1000 Litre Stainless Steel Water Storage Tanks shall strictly conform to IS 14333 / IS 15155 fabricated from AISI 304 / 316 grade sheets. The bidder must submit certified NABL test reports confirming 24-hour hydrostatic retention without distortion and non-toxicity compliance per IS 10500.
